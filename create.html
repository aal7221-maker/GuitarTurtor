Pick a video, give it the guitar audio, and your browser writes the tab. Nothing is uploaded: the song is saved in this browser.

Capture: Chrome asks what to share. Choose this tab and turn on Share tab audio. The video then fills the window, plays at 1× and stops by itself at the end (or press Esc); the tab is written straight after.

Running the local helper (python songforge/server.py)? Paste the link there to skip recording.

The pitch decides each note; the fretting fingertips pick which string and fret play it. Capture the video (the player fills the window while capturing) or use a video file of the player. The neck should be in view with the nut or a few inlay dots.

Grid wrong in the preview? Press Calibrate grid, then click fret A on the low E string, fret A on the high e string, fret B on low E, fret B on high e.
